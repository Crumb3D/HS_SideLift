# HS Side Lift changelog

7 Days to Die 3.2, 3.3 and 3.4. Install on the dedicated server and on every client. Restart the game after updating. Separate from HS Lift (vertical).

**1.0.21** — Runs on 7 Days to Die 3.4, and still on 3.2 and 3.3 with the same download. 3.4 uses the 3.3 model and network path. Also fixed on 3.3 and 3.4: the Side Lift Handbook opens again, and the setup tool click hint shows.

**1.0.20** — Stops, not floors. Since 1.0.14:

- Stops are numbered 1st Stop, 2nd Stop, 3rd Stop along the shaft. None of them is Ground. You can put several stops on the same floor height, at least one car length apart.
- An outside button registers to the stop it sits beside. The car does not have to be parked there.
- Two stops: press E on the inside panel to go to the next stop. More than two stops: hold E and pick a stop. A tap does nothing when there are more than two.
- The setup tool readout shows the active side lift, travel axis, stops, panels, power, and where the car is. New Side Lift, New Boat Bridge, and Use Side Lift Here are the only actions that switch which lift you are editing.
- When the shaft walls are high enough to reach the top of the cabin, the mod places a half-cube roof over the whole horizontal run. You do not build that ceiling.
- The handbook opens in a large scrolling window. Challenges and item text say Side Lift and stops.

**1.0.14** — Runs on 7DTD 3.2 and 3.3. Doors and panels keep their placed look while the cabin moves. Each outside panel belongs to one side lift. The no-power message lists what each panel is wired to. Cabin paints come from HS Lift, so the two mods no longer fight over the same atlas.

**1.0.2** — Cabin paints, and host-controlled cabin music that loops for every client.

**1.0.0** — Horizontal cabins and boat / ferry pads you build yourself. The mod moves them along X or Z.
