Craft the Elevator Handbook from 1 paper and use it in-game (not consumed). Same walkthrough: Journal → Challenges → HS Lift. After a new HSSideLift.dll, restart the game.

1. What you craft

ItemWhereWhat it is

Elevator Setup ToolWorkbench (electrician)Hold it, aim at a placed block (wood through steel, not dirt), hold E.
Outside Button PanelWorkbenchOne per floor, beside the shaft. Wire any one registered panel of that lift to a generator or battery bank.

Vanilla elevator doors for passenger lifts. Garage / roll-up for vehicle lifts. Half-block and plate-offset panels sit flush on thin walls.

2. Setup tool (hold E)

Aim at the block the command needs, then pick from the wheel. If you have more than one lift, choose Use Lift Here first so later commands edit the shaft you are looking at.

  1. New Ped Lift / New Vehicle Lift — always a new lift. Never overwrites one you already registered.
  2. Use Lift Here — bind the aimed (or nearest) shaft.
  3. Set Corner 1 / Set Corner 2 — opposite corners. Passenger: the whole 3D cabin. Vehicle: the floor slab only, both corners the same height.
  4. Add Floor — park the car at that landing, aim at a floor block, name it (G, 1, 2, B1, …).
  5. Set Ground — aim at the ground landing. That height becomes G; floors above become 1, 2, …. The car stays put.
  6. Register Panel — outside wall buttons only. Never the inside panel.
  7. Exclude landing — doorway slab stays put.

Corners name the car’s current height G. Use Set Ground afterwards if the cabin was already on 1.

3. Passenger lift

Build a 3D cabin. Smallest interior 1 wide × 2 high. Floor: any block. Walls and ceiling: plates and/or hatches. Mark opposite corners of that whole box. You need at least two shaft walls.

The mod places walls first, then floor, then ceiling, then interior, so the cabin does not collapse while it rebuilds.

Doors

  • Cabin door and landing door on the same opening open and close together. A back set is optional and follows the others on that floor.
  • Locked until the car is here. A locked outside door calls the car.
  • Cabin doors stay shut if there is no walkable floor outside (drop or a wall in your face). Outside landing doors are not locked by that check.
  • Hold E on the inside panel and pick a floor. A tap of E does not send the car.
  • No inside panel: hold E on the cabin door while standing in the car. Closing the door does not send the car.

Players, vehicles, and loot inside the cabin ride with it.

4. Vehicle lift

  • Platform only — both corners at the same Y. No cabin box.
  • One garage / roll-up per landing. Those doors stay put.
  • Pad here: E opens that door. Pad elsewhere: E calls it; the door opens on arrival.
  • Inside panel on the pad. Do not register it.
  • The truck and anything on the pad ride.
  • Do not build the pad out of Plate Double (pass-through). Use full cubes or any solid floor.

5. Plates, sheets, pass-through

Sheets (Wood - Sheet / billboard)Pass-through. Stay at the landing. Use around button panels.

Do not build cabin walls out of sheets — only the floor will move and the car will fall apart.

6. Power

  • Register only outside panels that belong to this shaft.
  • Unregistered panels do nothing and cannot steal power from another lift.
  • Wire any one registered outside panel. That powers the whole lift. Other shafts need their own circuit.

7. Calling, floors, doors

  • Outside button: tap E to call (or open doors if the car is already here).
  • Inside panel: hold E, choose a floor. Tap does nothing.
  • The car will not leave while something is in the doorway. Sheets and plate doubles do not count as blocked.
  • Doors auto-close after 20 seconds (real time) if the opening is clear.
  • Walk out while they are closing: they open again, then retry when you are clear.
  • Floor letter: vanilla wall letter/number within 3 blocks of each outside door. They show the floor the car is on, including while it is passing.

8. If it will not move

  • Finished editing lists what is missing.
  • No power, or the wrong lift’s panel.
  • Shaft blocked — it stops; it will not chew through your build.
  • Doors cannot close — wait until the opening is clear.
  • Fewer than two supporting shaft walls on a passenger car.
  • Restart the game after a new DLL.

Admins: console HSSideLift. HSSideLift debris clears dirt and rubble in the footprint (not the car, doors, panels, or pass-through).
